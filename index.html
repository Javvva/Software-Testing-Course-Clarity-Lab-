A little color knowledge

Make the most of your palette.

A few quick answers to help bring your next creative project to life.

How do I generate a new palette?

Click Generate palette or press the Space key. Choose a style to explore different combinations. Locked colors remain unchanged while the other colors refresh.

How does color locking work?

Click the lock icon on a color card to keep that color. The next time you generate a palette, locked colors stay exactly as they are.

What is a HEX color code?

A HEX code represents a color using hexadecimal notation. For example, #FFFFFF is white, #000000 is black, and #FF5733 is a vivid orange-red.

What are color harmonies?

Color harmonies describe relationships between colors on a color wheel. Analogous colors sit near one another, complementary colors sit opposite, and triadic colors are evenly spaced.

Can I use these colors in my projects?

Absolutely. Copy individual HEX codes or use Copy all to grab the full palette. Paste the codes into your design tool, website stylesheet, or creative brief.

Where are my saved palettes stored?

Saved palettes are stored in your browser's local storage, so they remain available on this device and browser between visits. Clearing browser data may remove them.
